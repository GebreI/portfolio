Project 1 / 03
Interactive dark fantasy story
2026
Zły Sen
editorialinteractive story
Zły Sen - "Bad Dream" - is a branching dark-fantasy story I wrote, designed and art-directed myself. The reader moves through biological labyrinths, cold mechanical corridors and crystalline chambers, and the layout shifts with them: type, imagery and pacing all change as the world does.
The hard part was structure. Overt and hidden paths, decisions that pay off chapters later, several endings including the protagonist’s death - all of it mapped before a single spread was designed. It taught me to treat a story the way you treat a product: as a system of states someone navigates.

Project 2 / 03
Brand identity · men’s natural cosmetics
2026
Manture
brand identitypackaging
Men’s grooming brands almost all reach for the same shelf: black, red, chrome, shouting. Manture is a natural cosmetics line, so that language would have been a lie - the brief was to look honest rather than aggressive.
I built the identity around restraint: a single-line mountain mark that says “less is more”, a palette of stone and slate greys instead of black and red, and typography that leaves room to breathe. The system covers the logo, a custom alphabet, palette, icon set and a packaging family - enough for a small line to stand on a shelf next to the shouting and still get picked up.

Project 3 / 03
Barbershop app concept · UX/UI design
2025
Zakład
Barberski
UX/UIbranding
A barbershop app has three jobs: show what’s on offer, make the price clear before anyone books, and get new clients through the door. Every screen is built around one of them - services sorted the way clients actually search, the price and scope of each visit stated upfront, and directions one tap away.
Visually, the concept breaks from the clean-tech look most booking apps share. A bold, gothic identity drawn from the shop’s own character sets the tone, while a restrained black-and-white UI keeps content legible - an interface that feels like walking in, not a template with a logo dropped on it.
